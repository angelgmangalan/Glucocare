# Physical activity, sleep, and stress

> Physical activity may help you lower blood glucose, blood pressure, and cholesterol levels. Being active may also help you get better sleep and improve your mood.

> Getting enough sleep may improve your mood, energy level, and blood glucose level. Most adults should aim for about 7 to 8 hours each night.

> Learn healthy ways to lower your stress, and ask for help from your health care team or a mental health professional.

These are brief attributed extracts, not individualized medical advice.
